THE PLAYBOOK

Simple hands. Clever echoes.

01

Catch with a swipe

Drag left or right to catch the smiling shapes. The arrow buttons work too. Circle, triangle and diamond shapes have their own tones.

02

Leave an echo behind

Each direct catch leaves a purple echo for about three seconds. Move away and let it collect another note for double base points. Up to three echoes can help.

03

Bring on the choir

Three identical shapes in a row earn a 60-point choir. After eight catches, tap Encore to collect every note currently on the stage.

Which mode should I choose?

Arcade: 90 seconds or seven misses. Journey: 18 chapters, each 60 seconds or seven misses; reach the score goal to unlock the next. Playground: no timer or game over, with slower notes and a wider jelly.

I cannot hear the game.

Check Game sound and Background pulse in Settings, your device volume, the silent switch and audio output. The game respects silent mode. Every gameplay cue is visual, so you can also play silently.

How do I pause or delete my progress?

Tap Pause to rest or go home. Leaving the app pauses an active game. Settings → Reset scores and journey clears the high score and chapter stars after confirmation. Removing the app removes its local data.

Accessibility and comfort

Shapes are distinguished by shape as well as color. Sound is optional. Settings includes reduced motion and optional haptics. Menus use native controls. The fast-moving playfield requires visual interaction; full nonvisual gameplay is not claimed.

PRIVACY POLICY · VERSION 1.0

Your song stays with you.

Effective for Gloop Orchestra 1.0.0. Prepared November 4, 2026. Developer: Yoojun Nam. Contact: givingtreemax@gmail.com.

Data used by the app

The native Gloop Orchestra app does not collect or transmit personal data. It contains no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, chat, analytics, tracking SDKs or network services. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo access.

Local storage and deletion

The app saves scores, journey stars and preferences on your device using Apple's local preferences system. Use Settings → Reset scores and journey to clear gameplay progress. Delete the app to remove its local data. Your device backups may include this data according to your Apple backup settings; Apple manages those backups.

Children and families

The app does not ask for a name, age, email or other personal details. It has no social features, advertisements or purchases. An adult should handle support requests without including a child's personal information.

If you contact support

An email you choose to send is separate from app data. The developer uses your email address and message only to answer and resolve your request, and does not use them for advertising. Email is processed by the email provider. Support correspondence is deleted within 90 days after resolution unless a longer retention is necessary for a documented legal obligation or an ongoing dispute. You can ask for access or deletion by emailing the contact above.

This website and Apple services

This page has no third-party analytics, ad scripts, forms or embedded media. Hosting providers may process technical access logs to deliver and secure the website. The website and App Store are separate services from the offline app. Apple's distribution, purchase history, device diagnostics and backup practices are governed by Apple's policies and your settings.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy answers will be updated before the changed version is released.
