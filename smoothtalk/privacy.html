Privacy policy

Smooth Talk collects no personal data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Settings and daily practice minutes stay on your device. Microphone audio is never recorded. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. A practice aid, not a substitute for speech therapy. Support emails are used only to reply.
