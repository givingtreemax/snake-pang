MIRRORAFT · 1.0.0 · Effective on first public release

Privacy & Support

MIRRORAFT is developed by Yoojun Nam. This policy covers the iOS game. It does not cover the hosting provider’s separate account or access-log practices for a web preview.

Your progress stays on your device

The game does not require an account. It stores puzzle progress, best scores, current sessions and preferences locally. The developer does not receive this gameplay data. The iOS app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or developer-operated network service.

Optional sharing and support

Sharing is initiated by you and passes a result text to the app you select through the iOS share sheet. The selected service’s policies apply. If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to that request. Do not include sensitive personal information in support requests.

Delete your progress

Open Help → Voyage settings → Reset local progress to erase your records. You can also remove local app data by deleting the app. Device backups are managed by your operating system and account settings.

Permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo-library access. Haptic feedback and generated game sounds are optional.

Contact

Questions, bugs, accessibility feedback, or privacy requests: givingtreemax@gmail.com. Include the app version, device model, and puzzle number if reporting a problem.

개인정보 처리 및 지원

MIRRORAFT의 개발자는 남유준입니다. iOS 게임은 계정 없이 실행되며 진행 기록, 점수, 현재 게임 상태, 설정을 기기에 저장합니다. 개발자가 이 플레이 데이터를 수집하거나 서버로 전송하지 않습니다. 광고·분석·추적 SDK를 사용하지 않습니다.

결과 공유는 사용자가 선택한 앱으로 텍스트를 전달합니다. 이메일로 문의하면 답변을 위해 이메일 주소와 사용자가 보낸 내용을 이용합니다. 설정의 진행 기록 초기화 또는 앱 삭제로 게임 데이터를 삭제할 수 있습니다. 운영체제의 백업은 해당 계정 설정에 따릅니다.
