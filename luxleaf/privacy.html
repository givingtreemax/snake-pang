Privacy policy

Lux Leaf collects no personal data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Saved spots (names and readings) and settings are stored locally. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. The camera is used only to read auto-exposure values to estimate brightness; no images are stored or transmitted. Support emails are used only to reply.
