Privacy policy

I'm OK Today collects no personal data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Only your check-ins (date, time, mood, meal/medicine/sleep ticks), your trusted contacts' names and numbers, and settings are stored locally on your device. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Reminders are scheduled on-device. When you pick a contact, only that person's name and number are saved; the app never reads your whole address book. Calls and texts only happen when you tap and confirm them. Support emails are used only to reply.
