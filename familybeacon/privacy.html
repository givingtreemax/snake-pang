Privacy policy

Family Beacon collects no personal data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Member names and emoji, plus encrypted locations, places and alerts (in iCloud); the family encryption key stays in each device's Keychain. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. This app requires each family member's consent. It cannot be used to track someone secretly; sharing is always visible on the child's phone. Location data is never sold or used for advertising. Support emails are used only to reply.
