Privacy policy

Sound Eye collects no personal data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Only settings such as speaking rate, text size and contrast are stored locally. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Camera frames are analysed on-device only; nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted, including recognised text. Support emails are used only to reply.
