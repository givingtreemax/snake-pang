Privacy policy

Ear Light collects no personal data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Only your settings and a list of alerts (sound type and time) are stored locally. No audio is ever stored. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Microphone audio is classified in real time with Apple's on-device sound analysis. Audio is never recorded, saved or transmitted. Support emails are used only to reply.
