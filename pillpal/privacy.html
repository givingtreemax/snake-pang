Privacy policy

Pill Pal collects no personal data: no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sent to the developer. Only your medicines (name, amount, note, times), your taken log and settings are stored locally. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Support emails are used only to reply.
